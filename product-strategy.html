Becoming the HERO

Roverpass becomes the HERO of the campground industry
through better campground management.

The How, What, and Why

As a two-sided platform, Roverpass serves both campers and campgrounds, but with a heavy focus on campgrounds, we can better frame the market through owners' eyes.

Better campground management makes for a better camping experience. When we solve problems for campgrounds, we make the entire camping experience (finding, booking, and managing the reservation) more efficient.

We identify two metrics that make clear how to enhance the owner user experience...

The Focus, The Approach

The full focus of the product strategy and development team is to enhance these two metrics below for campgrounds on our platform.

  • Increase Campground Revenue

    For campgrounds, we measure this by the number reservations received and average order value per reservation.

  • Save Campgrounds Time

    We can make managing sites and reservations more efficient by simplifying every process and using technology to our (and their) advantage.

1. Increase Campground Revenue

Primary Offering: Online Checkout

The Roverpass Online Checkout will continue to be the driving force of our product and business. It is the bridge we provide between camper and campground that lets us manage and enhance the entire camping experience. We will continue to focus on ways to increase campground revenue through the online checkout. Metrics: Click-Thru Rates & Average Order Value

Secondary Offerings:

  • User Accounts (Retention)
  • Hosted Websites

Future Offering: Distribution to 3rd Parties (TBD)

2. Save Campgrounds Time

Primary Offering: Point of Sale

The Roverpass owner portal should be used to manage every reservation coming through the campground. The Roverpass Point of Sale should be used to process every transaction at the campground, whether for a reservation, an add-on, or utility payment. Simplifying the functions of the campground as a business through these tools will make for the best owner user experience. Metric: Time to Complete Specific Actions

Secondary Offerings:

  • Availability Calendar
  • Advanced Reservation Processing

Future Offering: Long Term Reservations

By increasing campground revenue we will be the hero that helps owners build their business.

By saving campgrounds time we will be the hero that helps owners make their processes more efficient.

With full focus on our online checkout and point of sale, we will be the HERO of the campground industry.
